## 🧠 My Mindset

I believe there's a difference between simply using technology and exploring what you can do with it.

I enjoy playing games, but I also find it exciting to understand how games are made. I enjoy editing, but I also want to create the tools and systems behind digital experiences. I use applications, but I get curious about how they work and how I could build something of my own.

While many people focus on enjoying the final product, I'm also curious about the process behind it.

That curiosity is what keeps me learning.

I don't claim to know everything. I'm still learning, still experimenting, and still improving. But I enjoy challenging myself to go beyond what I already know.

I want to be someone who can do more than just one thing:

**I just want to keep discovering what I'm capable of.**

## ⚡ The Vibe Coding Era

I enjoy vibe coding — turning an idea into a working project by combining creativity, AI-assisted development, experimentation, and problem-solving.

For me, vibe coding isn't simply asking AI to generate code and calling it a day.

It's about having an idea, exploring possible solutions, testing what works, fixing what breaks, improving the result, and learning something new along the way.

AI can help me move faster, but curiosity keeps me going.

I enjoy the process of turning something that exists only in my head into something people can actually see, use, or experience.

Sometimes the result works on the first try. Sometimes it takes a lot of debugging.

Either way, there's always something to learn.
